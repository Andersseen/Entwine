# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Critique ideas and code, not people.
Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, and sharing private information
without consent are not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation to protect the
community. Reports will be handled discreetly and fairly. Contact the repository
maintainer through their GitHub profile for private reports; do not include private
information in a public issue.
